# Golden hole 5 under the S7 default: a sub-pixel tee move, a missed bend peak (2026-10-22)

## What happened

Commit 83deb52 changed how S4 breaks pointer ties: they now go to a named stage default instead of wall-clock timing.
Golden's accepted S4 variant became greedyOccluded instead of pinned. Hole 5's recovered tee moved:

| tee variant | centre (px) | axis |
|---|---|---|
| pinned | (484.20, 847.89) | 2.6170 rad |
| greedyOccluded | (484.50, 847.75) | 2.6146 rad |

That is a 0.33 px shift and a 0.14° turn.

The S7 default `routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchW` then judged golden 17/18, with one extra bend on hole 5. The pointer vote
fell back silently to routeMatchTeeAxisBadgePrior (18/18), so the state matrix still read "same".

## Which step flips, and why

S7 only, on the current golden attempt (`docs/evidence/dev-s1s4/ATTEMPTS`). Hole 5's candidate is Basket cf; its tee cap
S sits 12.8 px ahead of the tee centre.

**1. The K ≤ 1 sweep misses the bend's peak.** `kbends.mjs kCellAt` sweeps the bend B1 along the tee axis every 0.54 R1
(24.0 px on golden), then refines by halving from the best sample. Along the axis, the route's weakest leg is a narrow
peak:

| t from S (px) | 120 | 122 | 124 | 126 | 128 | 130 | 132 | 134 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| weakest leg (lum) | 9.8 | 16.3 | 23.6 | 27.9 | 27.0 | 22.0 | 15.1 | 10.4 |

The peak stays above 22 lum over only about 6 px. Its width is set by the edge band divided by the sine of the turn, not
by R1. Past the peak the reading is a plateau of about 8 lum.

Far down the axis, at t ≈ 209 px, the second leg falls inside the masked basket sprite and is unread. Such a route reads
its first leg alone: 22.1 lum.

Neither tee's 24 px grid lands on the peak. The single refine therefore starts from the far route and keeps it, at
22.0-22.1 lum. The cell's K = 1 route has a 110° turn and an unread last leg.

**2. The chain's polish lands on either side of the peak.** The K-bend chain seeds are B* (t = 141 px, weakest leg
7.8-8.9 lum) and that K = 1 route. The final polish is a halving coordinate search on the weakest leg, starting 21 px
from B*.

| tees | polish result | weakest leg | edge sensor |
|---|---|---|---|
| pinned | t = 128 (606.4, 777.2), the peak | 28.0 lum | 1.00 |
| greedyOccluded | t = 156 (639.8, 757.4) | 14.0 lum | **0.74** |

Which way it goes depends on about 1 lum of plateau noise 21 px from B*.

**3. The sensor gate decides.** With τ = 0.75, the accepted route's 0.74 fails. The hole is widened to ranks 3-4 and
the second-bend search runs. It accepts a 2-bend route: 27.4 vs 21.5 lum, sensor 0.93. The badge prior then moves the
first bend to B*. That is the extra bend at (647.6, 733.4).

Jittering hole 5's tee by ±0.5 px or ±0.2° shows the same knife-edge. The default keeps one bend, near B* at
(617, 770), in 8 of 8 jitters, but the accepted tee itself lands on the 0.74 side.

The fragility is real: a sweep step coarser than a bend's peak, refined from a single start.

## The fix (in the default)

New option `kRefineStarts: 3` (`kbends.mjs kCellAt`, `match.mjs`):

- The halving refine starts from the sweep's three best local maxima, not from its best read alone.
- A run of equal reads counts as one maximum.
- The sweep, its step and its reach assumption are unchanged.

`routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchW` carries the option, and so do its siblings that share its cells:

- `...WEvidence`: its cell rules must equal the default's;
- `...WFirstLegs`.

The extra cost is two more halving refines per cell, about 16 leg reads each.

Golden hole 5 with the fix:

- Under every S4 tee variant (pinned, greedyOccluded, greedyOccludedNoBadgeRay) and all 8 jitters, the bend lands at
  (603-607, 777-778). That is the peak, on Sam's corridor.
- The judged result is 18/18, bends 10/0/0.

## The state check

`ports/state/matrix.mjs s7PointerConditionOf` records a condition on the S7trace row whenever a course's S7 pointer is not
`S7_DEFAULT`. The condition names:

- the pointer;
- the vote's reason;
- the default's own judged result on that course.

It is loud in four places:

- in the row (`condition`);
- in the matrix emits (`s7PointerNotDefault`);
- in the markdown table and a block after it;
- in a closing line of `pxc state`.

On the attempts before this fix, `pxc state` printed: "golden: S7 POINTER IS NOT THE S7 DEFAULT: the pointer is
routeMatchTeeAxisBadgePrior (tie on judged result ...: fastest standalone); the default routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchW
17/18 agree, bends 10/0/1".
