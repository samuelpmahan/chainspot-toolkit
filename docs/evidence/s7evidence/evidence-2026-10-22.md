# S7 evidence: the earlier algorithms as perspectives on the default's cells (2026-10-22)

Sam (2026-10-22): "What happened to basket backwards? It seems like that could help with issues where 3/4lane freaks out
on reaching the basket. And could BE A GREAT WAY TO TAKE OUT EARLIER ALGORITHM AND USE THAT AS JUST ANOTHER PIECE OF
RANKING EVIDENCE!!!!!"

The S7 default `routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchW` stays the backbone. Its sequencing is now one store call,
`fn.q.trace.seqCells`. It names the candidate cells (hole, basket) and their routes: straight claims, top-3
neighbourhoods widened on reject, the K-bend search with the follower, owned corridors, priced turns, and no foreign C1.
Each perspective reads only those cells and records its reading in `tk.s7.cellEvidence`.

The sibling rule set `routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchWEvidence` takes the same cells from the same store call, so it keeps
every guard of the default. Only its final one-to-one assignment differs: it ranks the cells by the log of the combined
probability.

The default's output is unchanged. The end basket and every vertex match HEAD on all 22 courses: the 6 dev courses,
lenard1-5 and the 11 validation views.

## 3-4. Combination

The combination is a logistic, a perceptron with a sigmoid (`ports/s7route/evidenceModel.mjs`). It is fitted by Newton
steps with an L2 penalty on the weights (not the bias). An UNKNOWN reading adds nothing to the sum. The label is 1 for a
cell holding Sam's basket (BasketPoint within 12 px of his mark): 89 cells, 30 of them positive.

The feature set and the penalty were chosen on dev alone, by leave-one-course-out held-out log loss over five stated sets
(`evidenceEval.mjs SELECTION`) and L2 0.3, 1 and 3:

| set | L2 0.3 | L2 1 | L2 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| tracks: backbone, lanes forward, basket backwards (follower), meeting | **0.059** | 0.084 | 0.124 |
| every perspective | 0.066 | 0.081 | 0.112 |
| without edge / region / B* | 0.076 | 0.096 | 0.133 |
| backbone + meeting | 0.146 | 0.191 | 0.237 |
| backbone alone | 0.177 | 0.230 | 0.314 |

The weights `EVIDENCE_WEIGHTS` carries come from the tracks set at L2 0.3, fitted on all five courses: bias -1.507,
backbone 1.996, lanes forward 2.396, basket backwards (follower) 1.218, meeting 1.553. Edge, region, B*, follower
forward and lane backwards are recorded and rendered with weight 0.

For reference, every perspective at L2 0.3 fits: bias -1.225, backbone 1.749, edge 0.505, region 1.014, B* 0.194,
lanes forward 2.232, follower forward 0.131, lane backwards 0.234, follower backwards 1.202, meeting 1.333. Every weight
is positive in both fits.

Why the known-wrong holes are not fixed:

- **seatacFull 16.** Basket co, the short route, reads P 0.92: basket backwards reaches hole 16 and the tracks meet at
  6.7 R1. The default's cc reads P 0.94, because its route rides hole 17's hallway, the follower walks the same corridor
  both ways, and the tracks also meet. Every route-following perspective is fooled in the same way.
- **seatacFull 10.** It is an S6 straight claim, which the sibling keeps certain.

The every-perspective model was not taken. It also moved beaverranchFull 3 onto Basket cp, the basket the default gives
hole 20. The cross-view check reads that move as wrong (12.71 vs 10.62 R1), and it leaves 20 without a basket. Hole 3's
route rides its true hallway to a basket hidden under badge 4, which S2 did not detect, and then on to cp, so the
whole-route sensors (edge, region, B*) read it well. The dev selection had already dropped those sensors.

## 5-6. Rule set and judgement

`routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchWEvidence` is the default plus `seqScore: 'evidence'`; its implementation is
`impl/fn.Hole.trace/routeMatchKBendsSeq3SearchWEvidence.mjs`. Its cells are the default's (`cellRulesOf`, one store call),
and its final assignment maximises the sum of log P. A straight claim stays certain, and a cell the default forbids
(foreign C1, priced out, rejected) stays forbidden. It is not in `PROFILES.quick`, so the retest and the pointer vote do
not run it.

- **Dev**, agreement and bends hit / missed / extra against testimony, default = sibling:
  - golden 18/18, 10/0/0;
  - heritage 18/18, 18/1/1;
  - townelake 18/18, 3/0/0;
  - lenard 18/18, 0/0/0;

- **lenard1-5** (S0-S6 attempts made for this lane; no testimony): the sibling equals the default on every hole, and the
  default equals HEAD. They are 0-2 bent holes each.

## Renders

`node ports/s7route/evidenceRun.mjs --specimen S --attempt ID --out DIR [--renders all|changed]` writes
`tk.s7.cellEvidence.hole<N>.png`: one crop per bent hole with every perspective's route in its own colour, both picks,
B*, the meetings, a legend, and each cell's readings.

The judge is `node ports/s7route/evidenceEval.mjs collect|train|validate --dir DIR`; its result files are
`train.json` and `validate.json` beside this note.
