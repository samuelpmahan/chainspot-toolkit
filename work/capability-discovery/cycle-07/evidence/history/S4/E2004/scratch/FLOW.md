## Trace-only presentation and scientific limits

Both future CLI and Visual consumers join the same accepted trace by runToken/j/comparison/bank/original fit+control/new answer hash. They show all48 slots, original controls, derived raw arguments, per-side validity and values, native callbacks, skips/failures/unattempted rows and first/post errors. They do not recompute translation/contrast or access pixels. Display rounding never becomes a numerical input.

Primary non-gains can falsify a blanket nominal-sampling explanation; bright-control gains can falsify specificity; offset fit switching and masked invalid data stay visible. Fits, controls and contrast share exposed pixels and overlapping support: not independent confidence. Joint center+width changes cannot establish center-only/width-only causality, physical edge/path identity, calibrated width, gap/continuity or scientific/human acceptance.
